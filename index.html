Diploma thesis

Puzzle Assembly via a Robotic Manipulator

In progressPythonDeep learningRobotics

A system that photographs a jigsaw puzzle, identifies every piece, works out the solution and has a robotic arm assemble it. I'm currently building the vision pipeline. Each step below feeds the next.

images/thesis-1-input.jpginput photo of the pieces
01

Detect the pieces

U²-Net finds every piece on a downscaled copy of the photo. It's fast, and it only needs to know roughly where each piece is.

images/thesis-2-masks.jpgsegmentation masks
02

Segment at full resolution

Each piece is cropped from the full-resolution image and segmented again with IS-Net, so the model sees many more pixels. The result is fused with the full-image mask so that regions with background-like colours are kept. I compared U²-Net, IS-Net and BiRefNet.

images/thesis-3-contour.jpgclose-up of the refined contour
03

Sub-pixel contours

After mask clean-up (largest component, hole filling), each contour point is moved along its normal to the maximum of the image gradient.

images/thesis-4-corners.jpgpiece with skeleton, hull & corners
04

Find corners & sides

Positive and negative isthmuses come from the inner and outer skeleton. Tabs are cut at their isthmus chord, and the convex hull of what remains is nearly square. Its four vertices, snapped to the true contour, are the corners.

Next: matching piece edges to solve the puzzle, then pose estimation and pick-and-place with the robotic arm.

Optimal Football Lineup Selection

2024 · Python · Gurobi · Binary Integer Programming

Course project for Linear & Combinatorial Optimization. Choosing a starting XI is an assignment problem, so I modeled it as one for Olympiacos FC's 2023–24 squad.

9 / 11starters matched the lineup actually fielded in the 2024 UEFA Conference League final
  • 42 binary player–position variables from a 27-player squad, with formation, positional and single-selection constraints
  • Player ratings built from position-weighted Football Manager 2024 attributes combined with real SofaScore match ratings
  • Solved and compared 5 formations (4-2-3-1 scored best)
  • Integer programs have no shadow prices, so I wrote my own sensitivity analysis: rating tolerance intervals for each starter and the best replacement for each absent player
